2Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, rent or share any information about you or your device. The App has no servers, no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

3What the App saves on your device

To work, the App saves a few things only on your own device, where we cannot see them:

  • Your tinys (coins), mission progress, unlocks and settings
  • The profile name you type in, if any

This data never leaves your device through the App. It may be included in your own device backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us. Deleting the App, resetting your device or clearing its data removes this information permanently, and we cannot restore it.

4Read-aloud

The read-aloud feature uses Apple's built-in speech on your device. The App does not record your voice or send any text anywhere.

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting services and no third-party software development kits. It does not track you across apps or websites. Tinys are play coins earned inside the game. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for money.
