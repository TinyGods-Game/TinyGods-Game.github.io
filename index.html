Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Your tinys, missions, Sandbox levels, saved runs and run history are stored inside the app on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account and nothing is sent online.

I deleted the app. Can I get my progress back?

Sorry, no. Deleting the app also deletes everything it saved, and we don't keep a copy anywhere. If you restore your whole device from an iCloud or computer backup made before you deleted it, your progress may come back with it.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone or iPad?

Yes, if you set up the new device from a backup of the old one (Apple's Quick Start or an iCloud backup). Installing the game fresh on a new device starts over.

Can I buy tinys?

No. Tinys are earned only by playing: passing missions, reaching History goals, and the practice run. There are no purchases in TinyGods.

I'm new. Where do I start?

Open the Story tab and play the Practice run at the top. It explains every word and number first, then lets you try a short, easy run with tips along the way.

The read-aloud voice sounds robotic.

The game uses the voices installed on your device. For a better one, go to Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices, and download an "Enhanced" or "Premium" English voice. Then pick it in the game's voice settings.

Is the history in the game accurate?

It's inspired by real history, but simplified to make a fun game. Please don't use it as a source for schoolwork or real decisions.
